# Assignment excerpt supplied by the student

This is the assignment text supplied in the planning conversation. It is not the full rubric or the instructions for the four individual submissions.

> The final design project represents the culmination of your work across the different assignments. As currently envisioned, the final projects can take on one of three forms: 1) designing a sports-related wearable, presumably building on Assignment 4, 2) developing a learning experience for youth that integrates sports, technology, and learning, presumably building on Assignment 3, or Assignment 5; 3) using data analytics to model or predict sports-behavior or performance, presumably could be tied to any of the previous assignments. To help ensure that students are making effective progress with their projects, there will be four submissions associated with the final project. Final projects can be completed in teams of up to three students. The amount of work expected will be proportional to the number of participants on the project team. Additionally, there will be different expectations for each team based on the team members’ prior experiences with design of learning environments, analytics, machine learning and physical computing.

The student selected the indoor-bouldering video-review idea. The fencing idea was included accidentally and is outside this project.

The original climbing proposal envisioned hold detection/classification, body-pose tracking, observable-event detection, and timestamped feedback. The plan retains video-based review and analytics as the core; it stages automatic hold classification and technique diagnoses as later work so the first version can be built and evaluated.

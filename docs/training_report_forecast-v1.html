Experimental wrist-motion forecast

What this is: a Ridge model predicting where a tracked wrist moves relative to the hips over the next 0.5 s. Targets are later pose estimates, not human ground truth. It is separate from pose inference, from the event rules, and from any technique judgment. Errors are in torso lengths, not centimeters.

ClimbReview for Mac

Install
  Drag ClimbReview onto the Applications folder in this window. Then open it from Applications or Launchpad.

The first time you open it
  This app is not from the App Store and is not signed by a registered Apple developer, so macOS stops it the
  first time with a message such as "ClimbReview cannot be opened" or "Apple could not verify ClimbReview".
  1. Click Done (or OK).
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security section.
  3. Next to the message about ClimbReview, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password.
  After that it opens normally.

Needs
  A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

Your videos
  Everything stays on this Mac. Videos are never uploaded and no account is needed. Your reviews are kept in
  your home folder under Library/Application Support/ClimbReview, so replacing or deleting the app keeps them.
  To delete them too, delete that folder.

Something went wrong?
  Send the files in Library/Application Support/ClimbReview/logs to whoever gave you the app.
